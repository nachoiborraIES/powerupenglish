Level C1 Practice Questions Preguntas de Práctica de Nivel C1

C1 exercises require structural accuracy and stylistic sensitivity. Test your competence in rewriting sentences using negative inversion and academic nominalisation below.

Los ejercicios C1 exigen precisión estructural y sensibilidad estilística. Pon a prueba tu competencia reescribiendo oraciones con inversión negativa y nominalización académica a continuación.

1. Rewrite using inversion: "I have rarely seen such an extraordinary performance." → "Rarely ______"

Check AnswerVer Respuesta

Answer: "Rarely have I seen such an extraordinary performance."

2. Identify the diplomatic term for reaching an unbreakable deadlock where negotiations stall: (Sovereignty / Consensus / Impasse)

Check AnswerVer Respuesta

Answer: Impasse (or Deadlock).
