Level C2 Proficiency Challenges Retos de Dominio de Nivel C2

Achieving C2 proficiency requires instinctive syntactic flexibility and acute awareness of semantic nuance. Test your ability to reformulate complex sentences and identify subtle dialectal differences below.

Alcanzar el nivel C2 exige flexibilidad sintáctica intuitiva y un sentido agudo de los matices semánticos. Pon a prueba tu capacidad para reformular oraciones complejas e identificar sutiles diferencias dialectales a continuación.

1. Invert without using "if": "If you should require any assistance, do not hesitate to contact us." → "______ you require any assistance..."

Check AnswerVer Respuesta

Answer: "Should you require any assistance..."

2. Select the adjective with a positive, admiring connotation: (Obstinate / Tenacious / Pig-headed)

Check AnswerVer Respuesta

Answer: Tenacious — Highlights determination and grit in a positive light.
