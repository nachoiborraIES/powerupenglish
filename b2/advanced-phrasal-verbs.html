Idiomatic & Three-Part Phrasal Verbs Phrasal Verbs Idiomáticos y de Tres Partículas

At level B2, phrasal verbs become more figurative and idiomatic. Many feature three words (verb + adverb + preposition, such as come up with or get away with) and are inseparable. Mastering their register helps ensure you sound natural in professional presentations and Cambridge exams alike.

En el nivel B2, los phrasal verbs adquieren significados mucho más figurados e idiomáticos. Muchos constan de tres palabras (verbo + adverbio + preposición, como come up with o get away with) y son inseparables. Dominar su registro te permitirá sonar natural tanto en presentaciones profesionales como en exámenes oficiales de Cambridge.

Essential B2 Phrasal Verbs Phrasal Verbs B2 Esenciales

Phrasal Verb Traducción al Español Meaning in English Example
Come up with Idear / Proponer (idea, plan) Produce or invent an idea She came up with a brilliant strategy.
Get away with Salirse con la suya / Quedar impune Escape blame or punishment He thought he could get away with cheating.
Turn down Rechazar (oferta/invitación) Refuse or reject an offer They turned down his initial proposal.
Bring about Provocar / Ocasionar Cause something to happen The new law brought about radical change.
