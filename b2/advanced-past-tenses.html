Nuanced Past Forms and Retrospective Speculation Formas Pasadas con Matices y Especulación Retrospectiva

At level B2, you master subtle differences in past aspect. This includes using the Past Perfect Continuous (had been + verb-ing) to describe prolonged background activities with visible results, distinguishing between "used to" (past states and habits) and "would" (repeated past actions only), and using past modals (must have, might have, could have) for deductions.

En el nivel B2, dominas diferencias sutiles del aspecto temporal pasado. Esto incluye el Pasado Perfecto Continuo (had been + verbo-ing) para describir actividades prolongadas con resultados visibles, distinguir entre "used to" (estados y hábitos pasados) y "would" (solo acciones pasadas repetidas), y emplear modales pasados (must have, might have, could have) para deducciones.

Key Advanced Patterns Patrones Avanzados Clave

Structure Grammatical Function Example Sentence
Past Perfect Continuous Duration up to a past turning point She was exhausted because she had been coding all night.
Used to vs Would Past habits / states We used to live in London; we would visit museums every weekend.
Must have + Participle High certainty deduction about past The streets are wet; it must have rained.
