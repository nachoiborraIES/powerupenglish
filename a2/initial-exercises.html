Level A2 Practice Questions Preguntas de Práctica de Nivel A2

Consolidate your learning by completing these A2 exercises. Pay special attention to whether an action was in progress (past continuous) or completed (past simple), and choose appropriate connectors.

Consolida tu aprendizaje completando estos ejercicios de nivel A2. Presta especial atención a si la acción estaba en desarrollo (pasado continuo) o ya completada (pasado simple), y elige los conectores apropiados.

1. While I was walking home, it ______ to rain heavily. (start / was starting / started)

Check AnswerVer Respuesta

Answer: started — A sudden, completed action interrupting the walk.

2. I really wanted to buy the jacket, ______ it was too expensive. (so / but / because)

Check AnswerVer Respuesta

Answer: but — Indicates contrast.
