Linking Ideas in English Conectar Ideas en Inglés

Connectors allow you to move from short, robotic sentences to cohesive paragraphs. At level A2, the most essential connectors represent addition (and, also), contrast (but, however), cause (because), and result (so).

Los conectores te permiten pasar de oraciones cortas y telegráficas a párrafos cohesionados. En el nivel A2, los conectores esenciales representan adición (and, also), contraste (but, however), causa (because) y resultado (so).

Essential Connectors Conectores Esenciales

Connector Purpose / Propósito Spanish Translation Example
and Addition y I bought milk and bread.
but Contrast pero I wanted to go, but it rained.
because Reason / Cause porque / debido a que I stayed home because I was sick.
so Result / Consequence así que / por tanto It was late, so I took a taxi.
although Concession aunque Although tired, he finished his work.
