Narrating Events in the Past Narrar Eventos en Pasado

We use the Past Simple for completed actions in the past (e.g., "I walked to the station"). We use the Past Continuous (was/were + verb-ing) for actions that were ongoing at a specific moment in the past. Often, they work together: a continuous background action is interrupted by a short past simple event.

Usamos el Pasado Simple para acciones terminadas en el pasado (por ej., "I walked to the station"). Usamos el Pasado Continuo (was/were + verbo-ing) para acciones que estaban en desarrollo en un momento concreto del pasado. Con frecuencia se combinan: una acción continua en desarrollo es interrumpida por una acción puntual en pasado simple.

Comparison & Formula Comparación y Fórmula

Tense / Tiempo Structure / Estructura Example / Ejemplo
Past Simple Subject + Verb-ed / Irregular I watched a movie yesterday.
Past Continuous Subject + was/were + Verb-ing I was sleeping at 10 PM.
Combined (When/While) Past Cont. + when + Past Simple I was studying when my phone rang.
