Describing People and Places Describir a Personas y Lugares

At level A2, expanding your range of descriptive adjectives allows you to communicate with much greater nuance. You will learn how to describe physical traits (height, build, hair), personal qualities, and the atmosphere of places.

En el nivel A2, ampliar tu repertorio de adjetivos descriptivos te permite comunicarte con muchos más matices. Aprenderás a describir rasgos físicos (altura, complexión, pelo), cualidades personales y la atmósfera de distintos lugares.

Key Descriptive Adjectives Adjetivos Descriptivos Clave

English Adjective Traducción al Español Example Sentence
Friendly / Outgoing Amigable / Extrovertido/a She is very friendly with everyone.
Hard-working Trabajador/a My brother is a hard-working student.
Tall / Short Alto/a / Bajo/a He is tall and wears glasses.
Crowded Abarrotado/a / Lleno de gente The train station was crowded this morning.
Quiet / Peaceful Tranquilo/a / Apacible We stayed in a quiet village near the mountains.
