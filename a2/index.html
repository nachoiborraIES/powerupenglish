Level A2 Overview & Curriculum Visión General y Temario del Nivel A2

The A2 Waystage level consolidates everyday language and introduces narrative past tenses, future intentions, and comparative descriptions. Learners move from isolated phrases to connected, communicative exchanges. El nivel A2 (Plataforma) consolida la comunicación cotidiana e introduce los tiempos de narración en pasado, planes de futuro y comparaciones descriptivas. El estudiante pasa de frases aisladas a intercambios conectados con mayor autonomía.

🎯 Official CEFR Level A2 Descriptors:Descriptores Oficiales del Nivel A2 (MCER):

Can understand sentences and frequently used expressions related to areas of most immediate relevance (basic personal/family information, shopping, local geography, employment). Can communicate in simple and routine tasks requiring a direct exchange of information on familiar matters. Can describe in simple terms aspects of their background, immediate environment, and matters in areas of immediate need. Comprende frases y expresiones de uso frecuente relacionadas con áreas de experiencia especialmente relevantes (información personal y familiar, compras, lugares de interés, empleo). Se comunica en tareas sencillas y rutinarias que requieren intercambios directos de información sobre temas conocidos o habituales. Describe en términos sencillos su pasado, entorno y aspectos relativos a sus necesidades inmediatas.

Core Curriculum Pillars for Level A2 Pilares Formativos del Nivel A2

1. Vocabulary (Lexical Expansion) 1. Vocabulario (Ampliación Léxica)

Expands to approximately 1,500 words, allowing richer descriptions and participation in standard daily situations: El repertorio se amplía a unas 1.500 palabras, lo que permite descripciones más detalladas y participación activa en situaciones habituales:

  • Physical & psychological descriptions:Descripción física y personalidad: Appearance (curly hair, beard, height, build) and personality adjectives (generous, sensible, shy, talkative, stubborn). Aspecto físico (curly hair, beard, height, build) y rasgos de carácter (generous, sensible, shy, talkative, stubborn).
  • Jobs, careers & workplace:Empleo y mundo laboral: Professions, responsibilities, office equipment, work schedules (full-time, part-time, shifts). Profesiones, responsabilidades, equipamiento de oficina y tipos de jornada (full-time, part-time, shifts).
  • Travel, holidays & transport:Viajes, vacaciones y transporte: Airports, train stations, hotel reservations, luggage, asking for and giving town directions (turn left, go straight on, roundabout). Aeropuertos, estaciones, reservas de hotel, equipaje e indicaciones en la ciudad (turn left, go straight on, roundabout).
  • Health & body:Salud y cuerpo humano: Body parts, common ailments (headache, flu, sore throat, cough), and chemist/doctor consultations. Partes del cuerpo, dolencias comunes (headache, flu, sore throat, cough) y consultas en la farmacia o médico.
  • Leisure, entertainment & shopping:Ocio, compras y aficiones: Sports (play, do, go), films, music genres, clothing sizes, receipts, and store exchanges. Deportes (play, do, go), cine, estilos de música, tallas de ropa, tiques de compra y cambios en tiendas.

2. Grammar (Narrative Tenses & Comparisons) 2. Gramática (Tiempos de Narración y Comparaciones)

A2 unlocks the ability to narrate past events, contrast actions, state intentions, and compare items or people: El nivel A2 capacita al estudiante para relatar sucesos pasados, contrastar acciones en curso, expresar planes y comparar elementos:

  • Past Simple:Past Simple: Regular verbs (-ed endings with /t/, /d/, /ɪd/ pronunciation rules) and key irregular verbs (went, saw, had, bought, made, took). Verbos regulares (terminación -ed y pronunciación /t/, /d/, /ɪd/) y los verbos irregulares clave (went, saw, had, bought, made, took).
  • Past Continuous & Time linkers:Past Continuous y conectores temporales: Actions in progress interrupted by a completed action using when and while (I was walking when I saw him). Acciones en desarrollo interrumpidas por otra puntual mediante when y while (I was walking when I saw him).
  • Comparatives & Superlatives:Comparativos y superlativos: Regular inflection (taller than, the tallest; more expensive than, the most expensive) and irregulars (good/better/best, bad/worse/worst). Gradación regular (taller than, the tallest; more expensive than, the most expensive) e irregulares (good/better/best, bad/worse/worst).
  • Future Intentions & Predictions:Futuros: planes y predicciones: Be going to for plans and intentions vs. will for spontaneous decisions and general predictions. Be going to para planes e intenciones vs. will para decisiones inmediatas y predicciones generales.
  • Present Perfect Intro:Introducción al Present Perfect: Life experiences with ever and never, and contrast with specific past time expressions (yesterday, last year). Experiencias de vida con ever y never, y su contraste con expresiones temporales definidas en pasado simple.
  • Modals & Countability:Modales y sustantivos contables/incontables: Obligation with have to / must, advice with should / shouldn't, and quantifiers (much, many, a lot of, a few, a little). Obligación con have to / must, consejo con should / shouldn't y cuantificadores (much, many, a lot of, a few, a little).

3. Use of English (Phrasal Verbs & Conversational Flow) 3. Uso del Idioma (Phrasal Verbs y Fluidez Conversacional)

Learners connect sentences coherently and handle everyday social and transactional conversations: El alumno enlaza oraciones de forma coherente y se desenvuelve en situaciones sociales y comunicativas directas:

  • Daily Phrasal Verbs:Phrasal Verbs cotidianos: High-frequency multi-word verbs (wake up, turn on/off, look for, pick up, get on/off, give up). Verbos compuestos de uso diario (wake up, turn on/off, look for, pick up, get on/off, give up).
  • Sequence & Cause Connectors:Conectores de secuencia y causa: Storytelling linkers: first, then, after that, finally, so, because, although. Conectores para estructurar relatos: first, then, after that, finally, so, because, although.
  • Suggestions & Social Interaction:Sugerencias e interacción social: Proposing activities (Why don't we...? Shall we...? How about going...?) and politely accepting or refusing. Proponer planes (Why don't we...? Shall we...? How about going...?) y aceptar o declinar amablemente.
  • Agreeing, Disagreeing & Preferences:Acuerdo, desacuerdo y preferencias: Echo responses (So do I, Neither do I) and expressing choice (I'd rather stay, I prefer tea to coffee). Respuestas de coincidencia (So do I, Neither do I) y formulación de preferencias (I'd rather stay, I prefer tea to coffee).
