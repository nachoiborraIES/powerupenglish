Level B1 Practice Questions Preguntas de Práctica de Nivel B1

Test your intermediate knowledge with these targeted practice prompts. Decide on the most suitable verb tense and phrasal verb form for each situation.

Pon a prueba tus conocimientos intermedios con estos ejercicios específicos. Decide el tiempo verbal y la forma de phrasal verb más adecuada para cada situación.

1. By the time the manager arrived, the team ______ the presentation. (already finished / had already finished)

Check AnswerVer Respuesta

Answer: had already finished — Past Perfect indicates action done prior to arrival.

2. Could you please ______ my cat while I am away on vacation? (look after / give up / find out)

Check AnswerVer Respuesta

Answer: look after — Means to take care of someone or a pet.
