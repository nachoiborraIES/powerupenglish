Level A1 Overview & Curriculum Visión General y Temario del Nivel A1

The A1 Breakthrough level is the entry point to English communication. At this stage, learners develop basic survival skills for day-to-day interactions, simple introductions, and fundamental descriptive language. El nivel A1 (Acceso) constituye el punto de partida en el aprendizaje del inglés. En esta etapa se adquieren destrezas elementales para desenvolverse en situaciones cotidianas, presentaciones personales y descripciones básicas.

🎯 Official CEFR Level A1 Descriptors:Descriptores Oficiales del Nivel A1 (MCER):

Can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases aimed at the satisfaction of concrete needs. Can introduce themselves and others, ask and answer simple questions about personal details (place of residence, relationships, belongings), and interact in a simple way if the interlocutor speaks slowly and clearly. Comprende y utiliza expresiones cotidianas de uso muy frecuente y frases sencillas dirigidas a satisfacer necesidades inmediatas. Sabe presentarse a sí mismo y a otros, formular y responder preguntas sobre datos personales (domicilio, conocidos y pertenencias), y comunicarse de forma elemental siempre que la otra persona hable despacio y con claridad.

Core Curriculum Pillars for Level A1 Pilares Formativos del Nivel A1

1. Vocabulary (Lexical Competence) 1. Vocabulario (Competencia Léxica)

Learners acquire an essential core vocabulary of 500 to 1,000 high-frequency words covering immediate survival and concrete personal environments: El estudiante adquiere un repertorio de 500 a 1.000 palabras de alta frecuencia centradas en necesidades inmediatas y en su entorno más cercano:

  • Personal identity:Identidad personal: Names, age, countries, nationalities, greetings, and common professions (student, teacher, doctor, engineer). Nombres, edad, países, nacionalidades, saludos y profesiones habituales (student, teacher, doctor, engineer).
  • Family & relationships:Familia y parentesco: Immediate family members (mother, father, brother, sister, son, daughter, husband, wife). Miembros del núcleo familiar (mother, father, brother, sister, son, daughter, husband, wife).
  • Home & environment:El hogar y entorno: Rooms of the house (kitchen, living room, bedroom), furniture (table, chair, bed, sofa), and everyday appliances. Estancias de la casa (kitchen, living room, bedroom), muebles (table, chair, bed, sofa) y electrodomésticos comunes.
  • Time, calendar & numbers:Tiempo, calendario y números: Cardinal numbers (1–100), ordinal numbers (1st–31st), days of the week, months, seasons, and telling the time (half past, quarter to). Números cardinales (1–100), ordinales (1st–31st), días de la semana, meses, estaciones y lectura del reloj (half past, quarter to).
  • Daily life & food:Vida cotidiana y comida: Basic food items, drinks, meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner), colors, clothes, and basic weather terms (sunny, rainy, cold, hot). Alimentos básicos, bebidas, comidas del día (breakfast, lunch, dinner), colores, prendas de vestir y el clima (sunny, rainy, cold, hot).

2. Grammar (Grammatical Structures) 2. Gramática (Estructuras Gramaticales)

A1 grammar establishes the structural bedrock of the English sentence, focusing on the present tense and essential noun/pronoun mechanics: La gramática del nivel A1 asienta los cimientos estructurales de la oración en inglés, centrándose en el tiempo presente y los determinantes básicos:

  • Verb 'to be':Verbo 'to be': Affirmative, negative, interrogative, and short answers (I am, She isn't, Are they...? Yes, they are). Formas afirmativa, negativa, interrogativa y respuestas breves (I am, She isn't, Are they...? Yes, they are).
  • Present Simple:Present Simple: Habits and routines. Third-person singular -s/-es, auxiliary do / does, and frequency adverbs (always, usually, never). Hábitos y rutinas. Regla de la 3ª persona del singular -s/-es, auxiliares do / does y adverbios de frecuencia (always, usually, never).
  • Pronouns & Possessives:Pronombres y posesivos: Subject pronouns (I, you, he...), object pronouns (me, you, him...), possessive adjectives (my, your, his...) and Saxon genitive (John's car). Pronombres sujeto (I, you, he...), objeto (me, you, him...), adjetivos posesivos (my, your, his...) y genitivo sajón (John's car).
  • Nouns & Determiners:Sustantivos y determinantes: Articles (a / an / the), regular and irregular plurals (man/men, child/children), and demonstratives (this, that, these, those). Artículos (a / an / the), plurales regulares e irregulares (man/men, child/children) y demostrativos (this, that, these, those).
  • Existence & Modals:Existencia y modales: There is / There are, basic quantifiers (some, any), and modal verb can / can't for ability and permission. There is / There are, cuantificadores (some, any) y verbo modal can / can't para habilidad y permiso.
  • Prepositions & Questions:Preposiciones y preguntas: Prepositions of place (in, on, at, under) and time (at 5 o'clock, on Monday, in July), and Wh-question words (Who, What, Where, When, Why, How). Preposiciones de lugar (in, on, at, under) y tiempo (at 5 o'clock, on Monday, in July), y partículas interrogativas (Who, What, Where, When, Why, How).

3. Use of English (Pragmatics & Functional Language) 3. Uso del Idioma (Pragmática y Funciones Comunicativas)

Functional communication enables the student to carry out basic daily transactions and establish social contact: La comunicación funcional capacita al alumno para realizar intercambios sociales básicos y transacciones cotidianas:

  • Sentence building:Construcción de oraciones: Mastery of the standard Subject + Verb + Object word order and coordinating linkers (and, but, or, because). Dominio del orden sintáctico Sujeto + Verbo + Objeto y de los nexos coordinantes básicos (and, but, or, because).
  • Social formulas & etiquette:Fórmulas sociales y cortesía: Greeting, introducing people, saying goodbye, and expressing politeness (Please, Thank you, Excuse me, I'm sorry). Saludar, presentar a otras personas, despedirse y manifestar cortesía (Please, Thank you, Excuse me, I'm sorry).
  • Everyday transactions:Transacciones prácticas: Ordering food in a restaurant (Can I have... please?), asking prices (How much is this?), and asking for simple directions. Pedir comida en una cafetería (Can I have... please?), consultar precios (How much is this?) y pedir indicaciones sencillas.
  • Personal forms & spelling:Formularios y deletreo: Spelling out names using the English alphabet and filling in forms with personal details (address, phone number, nationality). Deletrear nombres propios con el alfabeto inglés y rellenar fichas o formularios con datos de contacto básicos.
